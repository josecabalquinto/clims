# Equipment records represent individual assets or batches

An equipment record may represent one individually tracked asset or a batch of identical items at one location, allowing simple quantity tracking for peripherals without requiring a record per mouse or keyboard. Its unique asset tag identifies the record; serial numbers apply only to individual assets, and items needing individual condition or location tracking require separate records. A batch may include both serviceable and defective units tracked by quantity, while units within the batch have no individual identities.
